What should a project enquiry include?

  1. The goal: What are you trying to achieve or improve?
  2. The users: Who will use the product or service, and where are they located?
  3. The current situation: Is this a new idea, an existing website or an operational system?
  4. The constraints: Mention devices, integrations, connectivity, privacy or accessibility requirements.
  5. The timeline: Share any important dates, budget range or delivery expectations.

These details help establish scope and identify a realistic next step. Do not send passwords, payment card details or sensitive personal records through the enquiry form.
