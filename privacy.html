Privacy notice

Last updated: 6 November 2026

This notice explains how the Kastine Augak website handles information when you read articles, submit a comment, send an article for review or sign up for publication updates.

Information you provide

  • Newsletter: your email address and your recorded consent when you subscribe.
  • Comments: the display name and comment text you submit, together with the article and submission time.
  • Article submissions: the author details, article text, tags, source links and optional media you provide.

Website activity

The publication records article page opens, reactions, download actions, share-button actions, print actions, discussion opens and comment submissions. These records help the publisher understand which articles readers find useful. Counts are on-site activity measures; they do not represent verified external social-media reach or completed printing or sharing.

Cookies and browser storage

This website uses essential browser storage and similar technologies, sometimes described broadly as cookies. If you choose a different display language, the site loads Google Translate, a third-party translation service. Google may use its own cookies or similar technologies to provide and remember translation preferences; those technologies are governed by Google’s privacy terms. It stores a random visitor identifier in your browser to help prevent repeated article and comment reactions. On standard site pages, it also stores a preference when you dismiss the cookie notice or close certain site promotions. These values are kept in your browser using local storage; the site does not need them to display its public articles. The AMP article copies use a temporary page-level notice dismissal and do not save that dismissal. The publication also records article activity in Supabase, as described above, to understand how readers use the site. You can clear locally stored website data through your browser settings. Clearing it may reset saved preferences and allow a new reaction from that browser.

Why information is used

Information is used to operate the publication, display and moderate discussions, review article submissions, understand site engagement and manage newsletter subscriptions. Newsletter delivery is configured through Brevo. Campaigns are sent only when the publisher explicitly chooses to send an edition; actual delivery depends on valid sender verification and service configuration.

Storage and access

The public website is hosted through GitHub Pages. Publication submissions, discussions, engagement records and newsletter subscription records are stored using Supabase. Access to private submissions, analytics and subscriber records is restricted to the two authorized administrators. Public comments are visible to all visitors. Subscriber email addresses are not displayed publicly.

Sharing and retention

The publisher does not sell newsletter subscriber email addresses. Information may be processed by the hosting and database providers to provide the website. Records are retained for as long as needed to operate the publication, meet legitimate editorial needs and respond to requests. Subscriber records can be marked unsubscribed or removed by an administrator when requested.

Your choices

You may choose not to comment, submit an article or subscribe. To request access, correction or removal of information you submitted, or to request removal from the newsletter list, contact kastineaugak@gmail.com. Please do not include passwords or sensitive personal information in a public comment.

Children

This publication is intended for a general audience and is not designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has submitted personal information, contact the publisher so the record can be reviewed.

Changes

This notice may be updated as the website's features change. The date above indicates the latest revision.
