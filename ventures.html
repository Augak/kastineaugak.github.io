Frequently asked questions about Llama GPT Deployments

What are self-hosted Llama deployments?

They are deployments of open-weight language models on infrastructure controlled by an organization, rather than relying entirely on a hosted third-party AI service.

Why consider a private or on-premises deployment?

Depending on configuration, it can give an organization more control over data handling, access, model configuration and network availability. It also creates responsibilities for security, maintenance, hardware and model updates.

Does self-hosting mean the system works fully offline?

Not automatically. Offline operation depends on the model, installed dependencies, hardware, network design and any connected services.
