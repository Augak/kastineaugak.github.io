About Kamulindwa Augustine

Operating professionally as Kastine Augak. CTO, Software Architect, Network Engineer, and Entrepreneur based in Fort Portal, Uganda.

Background & Professional Journey

Born in Virika Hospital in Fort Portal, Augustine's engagement with technology began early with hardware disassemblies and digital logic experimentation. He attended government-aided primary education before progressing to St. Leo's College Kyegobe for secondary studies. He further advanced his discipline in Software Engineering at ISBAT University, concentrating on artificial intelligence integration and enterprise network security.

Transitioning from self-directed development into formal enterprise systems, Augustine co-founded Apex Web Services and assumed the position of Chief Technology Officer at SomaGPT, driving high-impact digital solutions tailored to local educational and enterprise environments across East Africa.

A practical approach to technology in East Africa

Building for Uganda and the wider East African region means treating local context as part of engineering. A product should account for the range of Android devices people use, available storage and memory, network quality, data costs, language preferences and the confidence users have with digital services.

That context informs several priorities: keep essential pages fast, make the main task obvious, avoid unnecessary account barriers, provide accessible labels and feedback, protect personal information, and design offline support where the use case justifies it. A feature should be described as available only when it has actually been implemented and tested.

For organizations and independent creators, the same principle applies: choose tools that can be maintained, make ownership and costs understandable, and build a realistic path from prototype to dependable service.
