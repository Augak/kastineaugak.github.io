About Kamulindwa Augustine

Operating professionally as Kastine Augak. CTO, Software Architect, Network Engineer, and Entrepreneur based in Fort Portal, Uganda.

Background & Professional Journey

Born in Virika Hospital in Fort Portal, Augustine's engagement with technology began early with hardware disassemblies and digital logic experimentation. He attended government-aided primary education before progressing to St. Leo's College Kyegobe for secondary studies. He further advanced his discipline in Software Engineering at ISBAT University, concentrating on artificial intelligence integration and enterprise network security.

Transitioning from self-directed development into formal enterprise systems, Augustine co-founded Apex Web Services and assumed the position of Chief Technology Officer at SomaGPT, driving high-impact digital solutions tailored to local educational and enterprise environments across East Africa.

Recognitions & Credentials

  • Uganda Communications Commission (UCC) Digital Innovation Recognition: Awarded at Mbarara High School for pitching Soma Analytics, automating school attendance, performance metrics, and national curriculum compliance.
  • Asante Enterprise Challenge Framework Certificate: Received from the Asante Africa Foundation for operational business engineering.
  • Fortinet Network Security Certifications: Infrastructure operations and enterprise network routing credentials.
  • HP Online Learning Credentials: Specialized digital professional studies and cloud implementation pipelines.

Artistic & Civic Involvement

Beyond computing, Augustine is a dedicated multi-instrumentalist performing on the tenor trombone and saxophone with the UPDF Mountain Division Band. He also serves actively within the Uganda Scouts Association, balancing software engineering with public service and functional discipline.
